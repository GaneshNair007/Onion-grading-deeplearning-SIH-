# Design Contract: VOSTOK Agricultural Procurement

## Principles
1. **Physical Material Grounding**: Designed around authentic agricultural produce, optical computer vision, and acoustic resonance rather than generic SaaS marketing templates.
2. **Solid, Crisp Typography**: No washed-out or artificial `bg-clip-text` gradients on headlines; brand headings use solid high-contrast `#B8475D` or `#2D1F1A`.
3. **Restrained Instrument Indicators**: Machine vision and terminal states use functional status indicators rather than generic AI sparkle glyphs.
4. **Consistency Across Pages**: Landing story, interactive demo, and terminal workflows share the exact same palette and typography scale.
