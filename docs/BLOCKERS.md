# Blockers

## Official procurement policy

No authoritative primary document in the repository verifies universal Grade A and Under Relaxed Specification thresholds. Demo thresholds remain configurable and `source_verified=false`.

## Acoustic scientific validation

The available trained acoustic artifact is synthetic-only. Real browser recording, DSP, noise/impact gates and collection infrastructure work, but a defensible classifier requires independent onions with cut-open labels and grouped evaluation.

## Model readiness

Leak-safe detector and broad quality-class results are below production quality. The app exposes uncertainty and manual review instead of overstating accuracy.
